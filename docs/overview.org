* Purpose

~sim_lighttrap~ (branch ~vuv_meas_pmt~) is a Geant4 Monte Carlo of the LightTrap
*first layer* — a p-terphenyl (pTP) wavelength-shifting film on a UV-transparent
acrylic carrier — read out by a PMT in argon gas, as in the VUV bench
transmission measurement (liquid argon and vacuum are selectable). Its goals are:

- Model VUV photon (128 nm, LAr scintillation peak) absorption in the pTP film
  and re-emission at 340–430 nm.
- Quantify how much of the shifted light escapes the first layer (back face vs
  lateral edges, total-internal-reflection trapping) and reaches a Hamamatsu
  R6836 PMT placed 7 mm behind the acrylic, as in the transmission-spectrum
  measurement of the pTP-deposited acrylic.
- Provide an ideal-PMT reference (flat 100 % QE over 110–500 nm) against which
  the measured PMT response can be compared.

The two-layer design (blue-WLS slab + edge SiPMs + reflector foils) is developed
independently on the ~develop~ branch and is not part of this branch.

* Simulation Flow

#+BEGIN_EXAMPLE
Primary photon beam (9.68 eV, λ = 128 nm, Ø 14 mm, parallel along +z)
        │
        ▼
  World volume: argon gas (default) | liquid argon | vacuum
        │  (Rayleigh scattering / absorption only significant in LAr)
        ▼
  pTP film (2 µm)  on  UV-transparent acrylic (3 mm)
        │  WLS absorption → isotropic re-emission (λ ~ 340–430 nm)
        │  ← FirstLayerBackplane / FirstLayerEdges pass-through counters
        ▼
  7 mm gap (world medium)
        ▼
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  Hamamatsu R6836 (Ø 28.2 mm × 92 mm)        │
  │  MgF2 faceplate → photocathode disc Ø 23 mm │  → PMTPhotons / PMTHits
  │  flat QE (1.0 over 110–500 nm) per photon   │
  │  vacuum + opaque glass envelope             │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
#+END_EXAMPLE
