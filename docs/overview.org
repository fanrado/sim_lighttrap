* Purpose

~sim_lighttrap~ is a Geant4-based Monte Carlo simulation of an optical light trap
designed for studying scintillation and wavelength-shifting (WLS) photon transport
in liquid argon (LAr) detectors. The primary physics goals are:

- Model VUV photon (128 nm, LAr scintillation peak) absorption and re-emission
  through multi-layer WLS materials.
- Optimise light collection efficiency by varying the number and placement of SiPM
  photosensors.
- Validate optical surface properties (Vikuiti reflector at ~98%) against analytical
  Fresnel predictions.
- Quantify photon losses at the detector backplane and edges.

The simulation context is large-scale liquid argon time-projection chambers (LArTPCs)
such as those used in the DUNE experiment, where efficient VUV photon detection is
critical for triggering and calorimetry.

* Simulation Flow

#+BEGIN_EXAMPLE
Primary photon (9.68 eV, λ = 128 nm, VUV)
        │
        ▼
  LAr World volume
        │  Rayleigh scattering, absorption
        ▼
  pTP deposited layer (2 µm)
        │  WLS absorption → re-emission (λ ~ 340–430 nm)
        ▼
  Blue WLS Acrylic substrate (6 mm)
        │  Second WLS stage → emission at ~430 nm
        ▼
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  SiPM array (20–50 sensors, 3 mm × 1 mm)   │
  │  Quantum efficiency applied per photon      │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
        │  ~2% leak through
        ▼
  Vikuiti reflective foil (65 µm, ≥98% reflectivity)
        │
  Backplane / Edge leak detectors (validation)
#+END_EXAMPLE
